A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
jump in.

Curiosity is a good place to start.

How developed does my idea need to be?

Start with a problem, a potential customer and your proposed solution. You don’t need a polished pitch deck. More specific input gives the analysis a more useful starting point; gaps become questions to explore.

Is the Syncoro IQ Test a test of me?

No. It evaluates the venture idea across Innovation, Value, Market, Profitability and X Factor. The weighted score reflects the idea and the evidence available, not your personal intelligence or potential as a founder.

Does a low score mean I should drop my idea?

A score helps you identify what needs attention. A narrower customer segment, stronger evidence or a revised model may change the picture. In the sample, a pivot points toward a more focused opportunity and an experiment to test it.

Does the report replace customer validation?

The report is a starting point for it. AI-assisted public research can surface opportunities and assumptions, but interviews, experiments and paying customers supply evidence that research alone cannot.

How does Idea–Grants Matching help?

The Idea–Grants Matching Report helps identify grants potentially relevant to your idea. Use the shortlist to review each programme’s sector focus, venture stage, eligibility, deadlines and application requirements. Matching does not guarantee eligibility, selection or funding.

What can I download from this page?

You can instantly download the complete Freelancer SafePay sample as an HTML report and open it in your browser. To begin analysing your own idea, select “Validate my idea” to continue on Productica.
