01 — Executive Summary

The Structural Gap & Solution Engine

The Systemic Gap

Modern cultural preservation models are built almost exclusively on observation (museum curation) or theory (academic papers). This system fails to transfer actual craft mechanics to younger demographics, threatening thousands of indigenous folk forms with total erasure.

The Solution Engine

Living Arts of India™ transforms cultural heritage into modular, experiential consumer IP. Tactile craft kits containing authentic raw materials paired with artisan-led digital masterclasses and AR tracking create a new category — CultureTech.

Regulatory Alignment

Capitalizes directly on India's mandatory National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 structural guidelines, which explicitly require hands-on, experiential art forms within core school curriculums — creating a statutory enterprise acquisition pathway.

🟢 Investment Committee Verdict

Approved for Immediate Pilot Validation

Living Arts of India™ presents a highly defensible approach to cultural preservation by converting endangered legacy assets into commercial interactive property. Unlike legacy non-profits dependent on erratic public grants, this model creates an active product ecosystem where physical materials drive digital user engagement.

The strategy effectively leverages macroeconomic trends — specifically the statutory experiential education requirements introduced by India's NEP 2020 framework. This structural transition creates an immediate, enterprise-level customer acquisition pathway across private school networks, insulating early operations from volatile D2C marketing costs.

While the underlying multi-regional material supply line introduces clear execution and logistical complexities, the venture's high innovation index, robust competitive defensibility, and strong ESG indicators justify near-term commitment. The venture is cleared to progress into localized pilot programs to verify transactional conversion velocity across urban school test beds.
